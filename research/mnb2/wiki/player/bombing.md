# Bombing support

Bombing requests an aircraft bomb with later structure and explosion processing.
It is different from [close air support](close-air-support.md)'s scattered strike
effects. A request or a large damage number does not promise a chosen enemy's
destruction. [Selected 2.3.1g delivery chain](../bombing-request.md)

Advertised at **3 TP**. The selected offer first needs an eligible command actor
and Signaller, then the Signaller's later acceptance checks affordability again.
A career-dependent fee waiver does not remove those affordability gates.
[Request and payment chain](../bombing-request.md)

Historical scope: selected **2.3.1g** source. Payment, radio acceptance and
effect delivery are separate stages.

## Required roles and later payment

The command scan uses specialization 2, health above 10 and no invincibility flag.
It also requires global bombing and strafing state below 1 before finding the
specialization 6 Signaller recipient with health above 10 and no invincibility flag.
That links the offer to actual actor fields, rather than every radio-looking unit.
[Officer](officer.md), [Signaller](signaller.md)

An actor at exactly 10 health fails this offer's above-10 role test, even though
other named active/healing paths use at least 10. Compare the
[health-10 readiness tests](wounded-and-rescued.md#ten-health-does-not-mean-every-job-is-ready)
before treating a living or selectable role as sufficient for this call.
Comparison source: [Source identities](../player-notes/bombing.md#citation-1)

On a qualifying radio-completion branch, the Signaller gains 1 experience,
shared bombing becomes 1 and the 3-TP debit is conditionally waived by a separate
medal comparison. Initial sufficient TP is not an earmarked reservation, and
a zero-fee outcome still requires meeting the branch's later resource test.
[Exact staged conditions](../player-support-evidence.md#artillery-and-bombing)

## Placement and friendly-fire limits

The identified plane frame emits a bomb at initial x 20–280 and y 0–379 source
coordinates, then clears shared bombing state. The bomb later creates another
effect, which invokes a structure-destruction helper and an explosion helper.
These are distinct stages, not proof of a selected priority-target hit or a
universal 100 health subtraction. The archived discussion itself disputes some
priority/morale wording. [Full chain and community disagreement](../bombing-request.md)

No guaranteed aircraft arrival, elapsed call time, optimal placement or safe
friendly-fire layout is certified here. Already emitted ordnance, future global
support and the pending Signaller request have different lifecycles.

Sources: [Source identities](../player-notes/bombing.md#citation-2). [Exact source notes](../player-support-evidence.md)

## The blast is not limited to a 100-health subtraction

The bomb's damage argument is not a health-loss ceiling. Its core and fragment
routes have different recipient tests, and earlier structure removal can change
protection before those tests run. Exact outcomes still need actual geometry
and recipient state. [Blast arguments and recipient rules](../player-shared-bomb-evidence.md#the-bombs-numeric-damage-is-not-a-health-loss-ceiling)

## Detailed bomb fields and supplied health/distance examples

The bomb’s damage input is not a universal health-loss ceiling. Core and fragment damage, protection, recipients and ownership have separate conditions. The linked health/distance examples are conditional source comparisons, not actual layouts or survival probabilities.
