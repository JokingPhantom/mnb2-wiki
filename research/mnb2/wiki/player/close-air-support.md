# Close air support

Close air support requests **scattered aircraft strikes** rather than a
named-enemy attack. Budget 1 TP and keep an eligible Officer and Signaller
available; later radio acceptance still needs its own conditions.
[Exact request](../player-close-air-evidence.md#the-menu-queues-a-request-without-reserving-payment)

Historical scope: selected **2.3.1g** source. Payment, radio acceptance and
effect delivery are separate stages.

## What this call provides

The selected **2.3.1g** offer advertises **1 TP** and needs both an eligible Officer
and Signaller. Its menu checks each role's specialization, health above 10 and
invincibility state, and rejects while shared bombing or strafing state is
positive. This click queues a Signaller request without directly charging TP.
[Exact request](../player-close-air-evidence.md#the-menu-queues-a-request-without-reserving-payment)

The inspected aircraft requests strike effects across scattered positions,
rather than choosing a named enemy. Its later small explosions use their own
blast and recipient checks. This is a different attack from the 3-TP
[bombing request](bombing.md), and from the 1-TP [air-superiority state](air-defense.md).
Use the effect you need when comparing those calls; their prices do not establish
relative damage or vehicle-kill rates. [Effect and recipient evidence](../player-close-air-evidence.md#selected-aircraft-effects-are-positions-rather-than-target-selection).

## Keep the point available through radio acceptance

The later successful radio-completion branch checks that you still have at
least 1 TP. It then charges 1 unless its medal-based waiver passes, adds 1 Signaller
XP and requests the aircraft. A possible waiver does not bypass the balance
check. Spending your last point while communication is pending can leave the
request cleared without acceptance at that branch.
[Payment and cleanup](../player-close-air-evidence.md#radio-acceptance-has-a-later-balance-and-priority-check)

## The selected strike uses scattered positions

The retained aircraft has 17 guarded effect sites with separate horizontal
draws 20–380 and fixed vertical steps from 300 down to 0, with two final sites
at 0. They do not select a particular enemy index. **17 sites is a conditional
source count**, requiring each stage to be reached with positive strike state;
it is not 17 guaranteed hits or a measured volley duration.

The small-explosion path supplies AT input 2, fragment input 50 and damage
input 100 to its helper. Those inputs do not certify 100 damage per enemy,
vehicle destruction or safe friendly positions. Actual aircraft activation,
effect recipients, ownership and geometry remain separate questions.
[Effect positions and limits](../player-close-air-evidence.md#selected-aircraft-effects-are-positions-rather-than-target-selection)
