# Career gifts at deployment

Career ribbons can add **TP, recruit requests or free-order allowances** in the
selected historical **2.3.1g** initialization. They are different mechanisms. The deployment mode's
base TP expression is only one input to the eventual starting state.
[Exact source joins](../player-deployment-gift-evidence.md)

## Identify which kind of starting benefit you have

- **TP gift.** Adds to the balance present at that initialization write. The mode's base value and other initialization/cap writes; it does not set one universal final starting balance.
- **Bonus recruit.** Requests a particular recruit under its own flag/random/mode guards. Capacity and later load/arrival; a request or gift message is not a healthy soldier receipt.
- **Free-order allowance.** Supplies a field spent by a later order consumer. That order's menu, recipient or radio stage; the allowance is not an automatically applied effect.

The named examples below cover **selected historical 2.3.1g** producers and
consumers. Keep them separate from [starting stat bonuses](career-stat-bonuses.md)
and from an award being earned during a battle. These blocks do not establish an
immediate live-squad grant or durable saved profile.
[Exact initialization and allowance evidence](../player-deployment-gift-evidence.md),
[spending stages](../player-allowance-evidence.md).

## TP gifts add to the current balance

| Selected award               | Addition when its stored flag is exactly 1 |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Distinguished Service Ribbon | +1 TP                                      |
| Meritous Service Ribbon      | +2 TP                                      |
| Legion of Merit              | +2 TP                                      |
| MNB2 anniversary ribbon      | +1 TP                                      |
| D-DAY Ribbon                 | +1 TP                                      |
| Remembrance day ribbon       | +1 TP                                      |

With all six supplied flags 1, these sites add 8 TP. They do not set the balance
to 8. A supplied pre-gift balance 3 becomes 11 through those additions, with no
other writer assumed. This is a source-block example, not a guarantee of a
particular mode's fully initialized balance or later cap.
[Mode base values](deployments.md), [TP rules](tactical-points.md)

## Free recruit requests have their own guards

The selected bonus-recruit sites exclude Airborne Challenge's mode 99. Outside
that mode, Bronze/Silver/Gold Star flags feed one weighted-sum roll for an extra
Regular; all three supplied flags 1 accept 6 of 100 inputs at that comparison.
Westpoint's flag exactly 1 requests an Officer. Tank Killer and BBQ each use a
separate flag-threshold roll for a Bazooka or Flamethrower; with the respective
flag 1, one of 100 inputs passes. Those labels do not guarantee either specialist.

Each requested spawn still has its own below 100 player-allocation guard and
later load behavior. A gift message or request is not a certified healthy arrival,
and an allocation counter is not simply the number of living soldiers.
[Exact requests, selector mappings and limits](../player-deployment-gift-evidence.md#bonus-recruit-requests-use-different-tests)

## Free orders are not extra recruits or immediate effects

Xmas Ribbon's selected producer grants the Ranger allowance; Halloween Ribbon
grants Heroism's; Victoria Cross grants the ordinary paradrop allowance. The
selected consumers spend them at different menu, recipient or radio stages.
[Allowance comparison](../player-allowance-evidence.md),
[Ranger Training](ranger-training.md), [Heroism](heroism.md)

These are selected source producers and supplied flags. Full initialization,
actual random frequencies, delivered gifts and persistent save behavior remain
separate qualifications. [Progression](progression.md)
