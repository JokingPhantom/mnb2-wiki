# Choose the rules you are playing

This wiki covers two different games. Check the edition before using a price,
shortcut or combat comparison. A modern result does not measure the original.

## Historical Mud and Blood 2

Most articles describe the **selected publisher-associated 2.3.1g** reference.
Its SHA-256 is
`1e066cc28219fc68ba7a52604c6740d5f21d98332dbbe2466c45cc39c67a250a`.
This identifies the research reference, not the only authentic release.

Other archived files also display 2.3.1g. The Mochi-derived family differs in
targeting conditions, explosion inputs and a vehicle decision threshold. The
Kongregate candidate also has host guards. An old guide's version label alone
does not establish matching behavior. Earlier 2.2.6i–2.2.7a strategy claims are
not trials of this reference.

Start with the [historical beginner route](starting-checklist.md),
[controls](controls.md) or [versioned strategy studies](strategy-studies.md).
An original Flash player is not supplied by this wiki. If you use a separately
retained original, match its provenance and binary before applying exact advice.
No download, external host or account setup is required to read these pages.

## Modern reconstruction

The project's modern simulation has its own rules and corrections. When a gameplay build is attached, **Choose modern build and mode** in the hosted
project navigation opens its build/commit and mode selection. The selected build
and ruleset determine applicability; that entry does not launch original Flash.
If the link is absent, this host has no attached gameplay entry. The local
read-only wiki preview supplies no game build.

Read [modern rifle rules](rifle-skill.md), [unit lifecycle](unit-lifecycle.md),
[dated combat comparisons](combat-results.md) and [corrections](combat-issues.md).
The November 7, 2026 experiments predate later corrections. They describe the
tested stationary, unopposed packages, not current campaign survival or original
2.3.1g damage rates. Mixed articles mark their modern sections explicitly.
