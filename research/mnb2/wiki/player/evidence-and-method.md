# How to read the evidence

Use the answer and its edition label first. Evidence notes explain how far the
answer can be trusted; they are not extra controls you must learn to play.

## What the labels mean

| Evidence                     | What it supports                                         | What it cannot establish alone                                   |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Selected historical source   | Conditions, prices and branches in the identified binary | Real-time rates, every host's input behavior or campaign success |
| Retained playback            | The actions and visible results actually recorded        | All devices, destinations or unseen later outcomes               |
| Community report             | An attributed player's account for its stated version    | A measured success rate or compatibility with another binary     |
| Modern simulation experiment | The tested build, loadouts, seeds and conditions         | Original Flash behavior or results after corrections             |
| Tactical judgment            | A reasoned choice using the supported mechanics          | An optimal strategy or guaranteed survival                       |

## Controls and specialist evidence

The historical controls guide has limited retained mouse evidence for selection
feedback and movement. Hovering and selecting have different eligibility checks;
a destination marker is not proof of arrival. Keyboard labels are source/legacy
guidance with separate qualification gaps. See [controls](controls.md).

The [Sniper](sniper.md) guide distinguishes default stats, concealment and target
eligibility. A hidden-target rule does not reveal every enemy to the entire squad
or certify a faster attack cycle. Its modern clearance result is dated separately.

## Combat study method and limits

The November 7 expanded modern study used 65 supported loadouts across 17 infantry
roles: 4,160 finite-target trials and 3,664 separate grenade-launch trials. Each
expanded configuration had eight paired seeds. Actors were stationary, unopposed
and without cover; MG/Mortar began deployed. Veteran comparisons used XP/rifle
100/100 with natural kill progression active. These are controlled configurations,
not naturally recruited squads. [Results and actual comparison tables](combat-results.md).

The earlier retargeting and wounded-target comparisons are separate samples.
Counting grenade launches does not count damage or kills. A timeout or failed
execution does not become a kill rate; uncertain differences do not certify a
ranking. Full seed-level archives, intervals and raw logs remain local research,
and are not offered as downloadable evidence here. The article states the
conditions needed to interpret its published numbers.

## Intentionally unpublished material

Raw decompilation, private/local observations, source-identity ledgers, binary
catalogs and research work queues are not imported into this edition. A local
research citation identifies supporting work without promising access to that
file. This boundary also protects rights and private data; it does not mean that
every source claim has been verified in play. Historical media preservation is
separate from permission to distribute it.
