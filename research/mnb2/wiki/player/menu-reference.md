# Find the right historical menu

Applies to selected historical 2.3.1g. These are printed tooltip labels, not
certified keyboard behavior on every host. Use the mouse route in [controls](controls.md)
when a shortcut does not work. Opening a menu is separate from buying its offer.

## Main menu

| Menu           | Printed label | Use it to                                      |
| -------------- | ------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Reinforcements | 1             | Choose a recruit or placed weapon              |
| Commands       | 2             | Select an order and its recipient/target       |
| Logistics      | 3             | Choose equipment, then apply it where required |
| Radio          | 4             | Select support and complete its request steps  |
| Construction   | 5             | Choose a structure and finish placement        |
| Sound          | 6             | Toggle sound                                   |

Printed shortcut numbers are **not submenu row numbers**. Find the named offer
and read its tooltip; do not count down six icons to find Retreat. If the root
menu is showing, its sixth button controls sound, not mission ending.

The reinforcement button's 20-TP label belongs to airborne mode 99. It is not
a general fee for opening the recruitment menu. TP means tactical points.
Recipient eligibility, mode, career allowances, placement and actual payment
can differ from an advertised price.

For offers and base costs use [recruitment](recruitment.md),
[equipment](training-and-equipment.md), [construction](construction.md) and
[support](support.md). For the full cost of a role use [purchase budgets](purchase-budgets.md).
