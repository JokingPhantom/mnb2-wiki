# Pillbox

A **5-TP Engineer construction** with its own menu-side existence gate. A pillbox
can qualify for bunker, palisade and pillbox-specific overlap helpers; those
classifications do not grant one independent save each.

## Availability and payment are separate checks

The selected offer scans indexed decorations for an existing **pillbox type**
before checking for an Engineer with health above 10 and at least 5 TP. That scan
does not test structure health or opacity. An existing typed entry can block this
offer, but complete removal/replacement and global uniqueness remain unqualified.

Completion still needs the construction cue, a qualifying Engineer near the
pointer and at least 5 TP. The normal debit is 5; the selected Sapper commendation
roll can waive it after that affordability gate. A pending pointer is not a
completed structure or proof of free placement.
[Exact menu/completion order](../player-construction-detail-evidence.md#two-construction-paths-and-one-menu-side-existence-test)

## Protection and permitted actions are different

For an actual qualifying overlap, pillbox is recognized by the **bunkered**,
**palisaded** and **pillboxed** helpers. Each caller interprets its result.
A bullet save, a target exclusion, a weapon firing restriction and a repair
restriction are different rules. [Defense comparison](defenses.md),
[Mechanic](mechanic.md), [Officer](officer.md)

The German Sniper's inspected firearm has no extra bunker save. Nor does a
classification alone prove safety from every explosion or backblast.
[German Sniper](german-sniper.md), [blast/AT handling](anti-tank-and-vehicles.md)

The selected load clamps the coordinates it sees to x=50..350 / y=50..550. Its
hover-overlap path can also fade opacity to 20. Neither a visible interior nor
a faded drawing certifies an actor's transformed protection query or structure
health/lifecycle. [Physical identity and hover writes](../player-construction-detail-evidence.md#physical-identities-and-loadhover-writes)

## Some enemies exclude a protected infantry target during search

With a qualifying Pillbox query, the selected Regular enemy search rejects an
otherwise eligible **unarmored** candidate at its armor/Pillbox test. The German
Sniper search has no Pillbox query there. The enemy rocket soldier's ordinary
fallback has a Pillbox exclusion, but its earlier armored-first scan does not.
A light vehicle with positive driver+gunner also has a different Regular
candidate alternative from unarmored infantry.

These are fresh-search filters, separate from bullet saves and later damage.
They do not establish a safe vehicle position, universal invisibility, one-way
fire or cancellation of an attack already underway.
