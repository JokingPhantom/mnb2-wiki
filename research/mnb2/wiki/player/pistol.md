# Officer Pistol

In the selected historical source, the Officer starts with an **eight-round pistol**. Its shared-helper damage draw
is 0–74 health after an eligible unarmored hit; its own repeat and recovery
stages differ from ordinary rifle infantry. The community calls the equipment
Colt1911; the selected source table calls weapon 8 Pistol.
[Exact source](../player-pistol-evidence.md)

Historical scope: selected **2.3.1g** source. Modern findings, where listed,
are separate observations.

## What does the pistol tell me about a support recruit?

In selected **2.3.1g**, its close-distance roll benefit ends at 100 source units;
above 100 the roll is doubled, making the accuracy comparison harder. The
eight-round load and damage draw do not establish an Officer's firing rate or
make its command work part of each pistol attack. Choose the role using the
[Officer card](officer.md), then read the weapon rules for its shooting behavior.
[Load and shared shot](../player-pistol-evidence.md#load-and-shared-shot-are-not-the-ordinary-rifle-timeline)

Crewman and Mechanic also use the Pistol, with distinct recovery or job-selection
rules described below. A common weapon and ammo count do not make those roles
interchangeable. [Crewman](crewman.md), [Mechanic](mechanic.md),
[Crewman recovery evidence](../player-crewman-pistol-evidence.md#the-nonempty-recovery-expression-differs)

Distances are source-coordinate inputs, not meters. A larger modified roll
makes the comparison against rifle skill harder. With supplied rifle 10 and no
burst penalty, the accuracy comparison passes 21/100 initial rolls at distance 99,
11/100 at 100 and 6/100 at 101. These are conditional comparison counts, not
whole-shot damage chances or a measured maximum range.
[Declared inputs](../player-pistol-evidence.md#load-and-shared-shot-are-not-the-ordinary-rifle-timeline)

## Repeat fire has its own checks

The selected Officer continuation can initialize a 1–8 semi counter, then
requires positive semi/ammo and a separate experience-based test before
requesting another firing frame and reducing that counter. It is not the
ordinary Garand's 1–3 initialization or a guaranteed eight-shot burst.
A missed shared-helper accuracy comparison still spends a round at its tail.
[Preparation and repeat](../player-pistol-evidence.md#reached-preparation-and-repeat-requests)

## Empty and nonempty recovery differ

At the selected unpaused recovery action, empty ammo refills to 8 and writes
a counter of 200 minus experience; ammo remaining instead writes 100 minus
experience. Supplied XP 20 gives 180/80 at those assignments. They are not
seconds or the entire first-shot/repeat/retarget cycle.
[Actor recovery and guards](../player-pistol-evidence.md#reached-recovery-is-actor-specific)

An Officer also has command and aid work. The pistol's damage or counter table
does not tell you how often it shoots in a battle or establish an optimal
front-line role. [Officer](officer.md), [shooting stages](how-shooting-works.md),
[small arms against crews](small-arms-against-vehicles.md).

## The Crewman has a different recovery expression

A still-deployed Crewman also loads the Pistol and eight rounds, and its selected
early preparation/fire/repeat action bodies match the Officer's. Its later
nonempty recovery differs: **10 plus a 0–499 draw minus XP**, rather than the
Officer's 100 minus XP. At supplied XP 20, Crewman draws 0/499 write−10/489 while
the Officer's selected nonempty write is 80.

Those are source counters, not firing intervals or a guarantee of immediate
shots from a negative value. Empty-ammo recovery in both selected bodies writes
200 minus XP and refills 8. Crew replacement, later state and actual callbacks
still matter; one common weapon label does not identify every pistol cycle.
