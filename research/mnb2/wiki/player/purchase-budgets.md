# Budget for the recruit and the job

Hiring a specialist does not include every later structure, upgrade or radio
call. Use these **selected historical 2.3.1g base-price examples** to separate
the recruit from what you want it to do. They compare spending, rather than
recommend an opening or rank combat value.
[Recruitment prices](recruitment.md), [menu and role evidence](../player-specialist-evidence.md#recruitment-offers)

On this page: [Read the assumptions first](#read-the-assumptions-first) · [Building one position from scratch](#building-one-position-from-scratch) · [Preparing a radio request from scratch](#preparing-a-radio-request-from-scratch) · [Equipping an ordinary recruit](#equipping-an-ordinary-recruit)

## Read the assumptions first

The examples start without the listed recruits and add their normal paid prices
to one named upgrade, construction or call. They assume the relevant offers and
recipients qualify, with no fee waivers, free allowances, income or other balance
changes. They are arithmetic budgets, not observed completed transactions.

For exceptions to these normal paid sums, read [fee waivers, free allowances
and refunds](free-orders-and-refunds.md). Those mechanisms have different
admission, spending and recipient conditions.

If you already have an eligible recruit, use the remaining purchase's own rules.
A living soldier, sufficient TP or an earlier menu click alone need not satisfy
every later requirement. [Availability and payment](recruitment-limits.md),
[equipment receipt](training-and-equipment.md), [construction](construction.md)

## Building one position from scratch

| Planned purchases                      | Base components | Normal paid sum |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------- | --------------- |
| Engineer and one Engineer sandbag wall | 4 + 1 TP        | **5 TP**        |
| Engineer and one trench                | 4 + 2 TP        | **6 TP**        |
| Engineer and one bunker                | 4 + 5 TP        | **9 TP**        |

The [Engineer](engineer.md) is a 4-TP recruit. Its [sandbag wall](sandbags.md),
[trench](trench.md) and [bunker](bunker.md) are separate construction purchases.
Placement still needs a qualifying builder, pointer/proximity and affordability
conditions; a selected offer is not a finished structure.
[Price and completion bindings](../player-defense-evidence.md#structure-bindings)

Choose the protection category as well as the sum. Sandbags and trench feed one
ordinary-cover query, while bunker protection is separate for the shooters that
use it. The cheaper wall is not certified to have the same footprint or durability
as a trench, and none of these sums identifies a safe position.
[Defense comparison](defenses.md), [specialists under cover](specialists-under-cover.md)

## Preparing a radio request from scratch

| Planned purchases                                    | Base components | Normal paid sum |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------- | --------------- |
| Signaller and one Artillery request                  | 3 + 3 TP        | **6 TP**        |
| Officer, Signaller and one Bombing request           | 3 + 3 + 3 TP    | **9 TP**        |
| Officer, Signaller and one Close air support request | 3 + 3 + 1 TP    | **7 TP**        |

The [Signaller](signaller.md) and [Officer](officer.md) each have a 3-TP base
offer. [Artillery](artillery.md), [Bombing](bombing.md) and
[Close air support](close-air-support.md) then follow their own role, state and
payment checks. Keep the call's required balance available through its later
acceptance where applicable. Recruitment costs do not buy a successful call or
guarantee that every requested effect arrives.
[Artillery/Bombing stages](../player-support-evidence.md#artillery-and-bombing),
[Close air support gates](../player-close-air-evidence.md#the-menu-queues-a-request-without-reserving-payment)

**Smoke and Naval need different budgeting.** Smoke advertises 1 TP but has a
coordinate debit and a separate later acceptance debit in the selected source;
that is not a certified universal 2-TP charge. Naval copies the balance at
acceptance and sets TP to zero, rather than spending one fixed package fee.
[Smoke stages](smoke.md), [Naval acceptance](naval-and-counter-artillery.md)

## Equipping an ordinary recruit

| Planned purchases                    | Base components | Normal paid sum |
| ------------------------------------ | --------------- | --------------- |
| Regular Soldier and Garand upgrade   | 3 + 2 TP        | **5 TP**        |
| Regular Soldier and Carbine upgrade  | 3 + 2 TP        | **5 TP**        |
| Regular Soldier and Thompson upgrade | 3 + 3 TP        | **6 TP**        |
| Regular Soldier and Shotgun upgrade  | 3 + 1 TP        | **4 TP**        |
| Regular Soldier and BAR upgrade      | 3 + 6 TP        | **9 TP**        |
| Regular Soldier and Ranger Training  | 3 + 5 TP        | **8 TP**        |

These weapon sums use the selected [equipment-menu prices](weapon-comparison.md#which-weapon-offers-can-i-budget-for),
under the normal paid assumptions above. Equal 5-TP totals for a recruit plus
Garand or Carbine do not make those weapons equivalent. A weapon price also
does not include training, grenades or a guaranteed magazine refill.
[Weapon tradeoffs](weapon-comparison.md), [purchase versus refill](weapon-comparison.md#does-buying-or-rebuying-a-weapon-refill-ammunition)

If the recipient already has the pending weapon, the selected ordinary/Scout
handler has a stored-cost credit instead of another weapon assignment. The
table is a budget for the intended new loadout, not proof every click ultimately
spends that sum. [Duplicate-credit branch](free-orders-and-refunds.md#a-duplicate-weapon-has-its-own-guarded-credit)

The [Regular Soldier](regular-soldier.md), [Garand](m1-garand.md) and
[Ranger Training](ranger-training.md) have these base offers, but the outcomes
are different. A qualifying Regular/Scout training receipt also grants grenade
capability and adds separate 1–15 rifle/morale draws, while replacing the weapon
with Garand. The Garand purchase alone is a weapon replacement, rather than
those training benefits. This cost comparison does not measure kills per TP.
Career-supplied starting equipment can also make a listed weapon purchase
unnecessary; inspect the actual recruit before applying another upgrade.
[Weapon offers](../player-weapon-evidence.md#weapon-offers),
[training receipt](../ranger-training.md#explicit-regularscout-and-sniper-receipts)

Apply pending choices separately and check the recipient. An earlier pending
choice can take the click, and training can overwrite a weapon already bought.
No universal cancellation or refund follows from adding the advertised prices.
[Equipment steps](training-and-equipment.md), [replacement consequence](ranger-training.md#will-training-keep-a-bar-i-already-bought)
