# Recruitment and choosing a role

Choose a recruit in the Reinforcements menu for the job you need: fighting,
healing, command, construction, repair or radio support.

Applies to **historical selected 2.3.1g**. Prices below are **base advertised
tactical points (TP)**. Check availability and payment exceptions before buying;
a menu label does not guarantee successful arrival. Four paratroopers are a
grouped call, not a 12-TP single recruit.
[Price and offer evidence](../player-specialist-evidence.md#recruitment-offers)

Planning a structure, radio call or upgrade as well as the recruit? Compare
[recruit-plus-job budgets](purchase-budgets.md). Hiring the role and paying for
its later action are separate stages.

## Choose a complete commitment

For **selected historical 2.3.1g**, compare the job you can finish, not only the
first recruit price. These normal paid examples assume no listed recruit is
already present, eligible offers/recipients and no waiver or free allowance.

| Problem now                       | Normal paid commitment                      | Tradeoff to inspect                                                                              |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Add an ordinary equipped rifleman | Regular Soldier 3 + Garand 2 = **5 TP**     | A weapon purchase is not training, a guaranteed refill or a measured damage ranking.             |
| Build basic ordinary cover        | Engineer 4 + sandbag wall 1 = **5 TP**      | Placement must complete; another ordinary-cover structure does not add another independent save. |
| Add mobile anti-armor fire        | Bazooka soldier = **4 TP**                  | Check readiness and backblast; hiring it does not guarantee a vehicle hit.                       |
| Prepare one Artillery request     | Signaller 3 + call 3 = **6 TP**             | Request acceptance, balance and later delivery remain separate.                                  |
| Prepare one Bombing request       | Officer 3 + Signaller 3 + call 3 = **9 TP** | Hiring both roles commits TP before the call can be attempted; arrival is not guaranteed.        |

With an existing eligible role, compare the **remaining** action cost instead.
The full [purchase-budget assumptions](purchase-budgets.md) explain the sums.
Use the [three adaptable plans](strategy-studies.md) to decide when protection,
armor response or a career goal changes the next purchase. For another ruleset,
check [edition identity](editions.md).

## Availability and payment can change the offer

The inspected direct recruitment buttons check affordability before their
possible medal fee waiver. Sarge has a deployment-flag gate, the Resistance
button can substitute a Spec Ops template, and ordinary radio paratroopers have
request and later acceptance checks. [Recruitment limits and examples](recruitment-limits.md)

There is no single price-to-rifle ladder. An Officer's command gate, a Medic's
multi-patient sweep and a Signaller's request path do different jobs. Inspect the
actual recruit's stats and current career bonuses rather than treating a higher
price as a strict combat upgrade. Specialist actor weapon fields also do not
always belong to the shared infantry-weapon namespace.

## Before equipping a recruit

Weapon and training purchases can create pending selections that need a later
eligible recipient click. Earlier grenade/order/training selections can take
precedence, and replacing equipment can overwrite its old value. The existing
Garand and training explanations give bounded examples; they do not certify
universal cancellation or refunds. [Garand](m1-garand.md),
[Ranger Training](../ranger-training.md), [equipment receipt](../bar.md)

## Are support recruits also extra riflemen?

Check the **role's actual actions**, not just a weapon label or rifle stat.
These selected **2.3.1g** examples have different combat and job conditions:

| Role                    | What to expect from the retained source paths                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Medic](medic.md)       | The inspected own controller and six own action bodies have no ordinary enemy search or shared firing call. Do not budget it as a spare rifleman. |
| [Officer](officer.md)   | Has its own Pistol firing path; command access and automatic aid are separate jobs with separate conditions.                                      |
| [Crewman](crewman.md)   | Can use its Pistol while it remains deployed. A successful supported crew-entry branch removes that Crewman from the field.                       |
| [Mechanic](mechanic.md) | Has a Pistol path, but a successful repair takes priority over a fresh enemy search in the inspected scan.                                        |
| [Engineer](engineer.md) | Starts with a Grease Gun and has retained firing/repeat actions. This does not qualify when combat resumes after building or defusing.            |

Exact evidence: [Medic action scope](../player-medic-controller-evidence.md),
[Officer Pistol](../player-pistol-evidence.md),
[Crewman Pistol](../player-crewman-pistol-evidence.md),
[Mechanic repair/search choice](../player-mechanic-combat-evidence.md#a-successful-repair-scan-takes-priority-over-new-acquisition),
[Engineer combat bindings](../player-engineer-combat-evidence.md).

A shared Pistol does not give Officer, Crewman and Mechanic the same recovery
rules. Nor do these examples establish that any support job and attack happen
at once, that every recruit accepts the same upgrades, or which role gives the
most kills per TP. Read the role's own state and recipient conditions before
substituting it for ordinary infantry. [Pistol role comparison](pistol.md).

## A random-stop rifle load is not five fixed additions

Four inspected soldier loads start their loop counter at 0, add 1–10 rifle, then
replace the counter with 0–9; values below 5 continue. The first addition is
admitted, and 5 is the stopping threshold. Under separately supplied independent
uniform inputs, that loop averages two additions, or 11 rifle points before
career bonuses. This gives conditional means 21 for the selected Regular/Engineer
base 10, 31 for Paratrooper base 20 and 41 for Sarge base 30.

Those are declared mathematical means, not measured recruits or final hit
percentages. A longer source input sequence can exceed the range of five fixed
rolls; the inspected loop has no local maximum-roll counter or rifle clamp.
Actual birth, later load bonuses and readers still need their own bindings.
[Exact four-body loop and input boundaries](../player-random-stop-load-evidence.md).
