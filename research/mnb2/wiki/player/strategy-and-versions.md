# Using historical strategies with the right version

Treat an old strategy as a **versioned hypothesis with a starting profile and
deployment**, rather than a promised success rate. Two files can both display
**2.3.1g** while containing different rules; career state and mode can change an
opening before the first recruit is bought. [Version evidence](editions.md)

## Turn a historical idea into a present decision

The [three adaptable plans](strategy-studies.md) turn the available evidence
into bounded choices: protect an infantry position, answer armor while planning
a vehicle transition, or track a career goal with compatible purchases. Each
names the selected edition, starting assumptions, full paid commitments,
adaptation triggers and failure cases. They distinguish tactical judgment from
community anecdotes and report that no new strategy trial was run.

Use those plans for decisions and this page for checking an old guide's
assumptions. The [edition guide](editions.md) separates original binary families
from current modern play and archived combat measurements.

## Check which game and starting conditions the guide assumes

The selected publisher-associated reference differs from the same-labelled
Mochi-derived family in targeting conditions, explosion inputs and a vehicle
random-decision threshold. The local Kongregate candidate has additional host
guards. A version label alone does not identify one exact behavior profile.
The reference hash is in the [source notes](../player-reference-evidence.md).

A recovered wave-100 guide carrying a 95% title claim explicitly names
**2.2.6i–2.2.7a** and is categorized as outdated. Its title does not provide a
trial count, failure log or statistical success estimate for selected 2.3.1g.
Its themes—experience development, suppression, fortification and anti-tank
preparation—can still be studied with that context.
[RedxWing case](../redxwing-case.md)

The post-2.3 Tank Fortress account instead explains a move toward tanks, the .50
upgrade and staged utility/defense dependencies. Its author-reported long run
and award viability remain claims, not a tested transition plan for every
starting rank or version. [Tank Fortress case](../tank-fortress-case.md)

## Compare outcomes with the inputs attached

A useful future trial states exact binary/profile, deployment, career bonuses,
starting resources, placements, control sequence and how failures are counted.
Record losses and incomplete transitions alongside long runs. Per-visit source
probabilities do not become per-second rates without scheduling evidence.

For example, even an assumed independent 160/3000 chance repeated 19 times gives
about 65% chance of at least one event, not certainty:
1 − (1 − 160/3000)^19 ≈ 0.65. Here 19 is the supplied number of independent
opportunities, not a measured number of game checks. That arithmetic does not
establish the game's check cadence. [Six-unit probability comparison](../six-man-rule.md)
