# Three plans to adapt during a battle

Choose a plan for the problem you have now: keep an infantry position working,
answer armor, or pursue a career goal without confusing its counters. These are
**tactical judgments grounded in the selected historical 2.3.1g rules**, not
recorded successful runs, optimal build orders or measured survival rates.
Modern experiments have [different limits](combat-results.md).

All three plans assume **Default deployment**, the selected publisher-associated
2.3.1g reference and an inspected career profile. Default initialization requests
four Regular Soldiers and assigns base TP 0 before later additions; verify actual
arrivals, equipment, bonuses and displayed TP. For the purchase examples, assume
no relevant fee waiver, free allowance or extra starting specialist. The sums
describe additional normal paid purchases once you have the balance, not an
immediately affordable opening. [Starting conditions](deployments.md),
[purchase assumptions](purchase-budgets.md#read-the-assumptions-first).

## Hold an infantry position without disabling its support

**Goal:** protect the infantry already present while retaining a usable response
to casualties or armor. This plan spends on protection before another expensive
rifle package; that is a choice about the immediate threat, not a combat ranking.

1. Inspect the four requested starting soldiers and identify who is actually
   firing, wounded or pinned. [Controls](controls.md) and the
   [nonfiring checklist](why-soldiers-stop-shooting.md) distinguish these problems.
2. When 5 TP is available, an **Engineer (4) + one sandbag wall (1)** is a
   normal paid commitment. Use an eligible builder and complete placement;
   selecting the offer alone does not create the wall.
3. Place the intended rifle position in qualifying ordinary cover. Keep a
   specialist's work position a separate decision: a Mechanic's repair entry
   excludes bunker/palisade classifications, while the Medic has different
   gates. A useful-looking shelter does not prove an ability remains usable.
4. Reassess the threat before expanding. Another 5-TP bunker purchase would
   make **10 TP total** with the Engineer and wall. It can add a separate save
   where the relevant queries succeed; adding a 2-TP trench instead makes
   **7 TP total**, but wall and trench still feed one ordinary-cover save.

Conceptual position labels, **not scale or certified protection geometry**:

```text
Enemy approach
      ↓
[A: intended rifle position / ordinary cover]

[B: builder or aid position, checked for its own ability]
[C: room to relocate if explosives or armor threaten A]
```

At A, verify the actual protected point rather than trusting visible overlap.
B is not automatically a safe or in-range position. C represents an option to
change the arrangement, not a measured escape distance. See the
[instructional diagrams](instructional-visuals.md).

**Adapt when:** repeated wounds suggest the current defense does not answer the
attacker; a specialist stops working; an explosive or vehicle threatens the
position. Check the attacker-specific exception and the unit's action first.
Do not keep buying the same cover category expecting an extra independent save.
[Defense comparison](defenses.md), [specialists under cover](specialists-under-cover.md).

**Failure cases:** a blast can affect structures and occupants; enemy Snipers
and SS have different save behavior; pinning or action state can still stop a
protected soldier. The conditional save table measures none of whole-squad
survival, building durability or a safe explosive spacing.

The older RedxWing guide's experience/fortification themes are community context
for **2.2.6i–2.2.7a**, not validation of this selected-2.3.1g plan. Its title's
95% claim has no recovered trial denominator. [Versioned strategy context](strategy-and-versions.md).

## Answer armor before committing to a tank transition

**Goal:** acquire an anti-armor capability while keeping its firing and friendly
hazard requirements visible. Choose by the current gap, not by explosion size.

| Available additional TP | Candidate commitment                                                       | What it requires or gives up                                                                                 |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 4                       | One [Bazooka soldier](bazooka.md)                                          | Readiness, acquisition and rocket travel still matter; keep its backblast hazard away from unarmored allies. |
| 5                       | [Engineer](engineer.md) 4 + one [AT mine](mines.md) 1                      | A placed trigger rather than a mobile shooter; friendly armored vehicles can also meet the trigger filter.   |
| 8                       | One [M3 AT gun](m3-at-gun.md)                                              | Check crew, line of sight and nearby M3 spacing; crew replacement/repair are separate costs.                 |
| 20                      | One [Sherman](sherman.md)                                                  | Large up-front commitment; damaged health, lost crew and optional upgrades are separate jobs and costs.      |
| 24                      | [Halftrack](halftrack.md) 12 + [75 mm conversion](halftrack-weapons.md) 12 | Replaces the module role; its selected target scan requires distance strictly above 400 source units.        |

These are normal paid base commitments without prior listed units. With an
existing eligible Engineer, the incremental mine price is 1 TP. A 2-TP sticky
bomb still needs an eligible Paratrooper; buying that capability from scratch
is not a universal 2-TP plan. [All AT options and prerequisites](anti-tank-options.md).

1. Identify whether the immediate failure is **no eligible target**, **no firing
   request**, **rocket/effect placement**, or **vehicle damage after the effect**.
   [AT stages](anti-tank-and-vehicles.md) separates these checks.
2. Choose a capability you can fund through its actual completion. An M3's
   armor-priority pass does not inherit the distant-target fallback threshold;
   a Halftrack AT conversion does. Avoid buying the latter as an answer to a
   close threat solely because both are called AT.
3. Treat the firing line, nearby infantry and vehicle route as separate spaces:
   **A** is the shooter, **B** its intended enemy area, **C** the allies or
   friendly-vehicle route that must be checked for backblast, scatter or mines.
   Those labels do not establish a safe radius. Do not put a mine on C and
   assume ownership prevents a friendly armored trigger.
4. If planning a Sherman later, account for what answers armor while saving.
   A prior 4-TP Bazooka plus a later 20-TP Sherman is **24 TP across two purchases**,
   before repair, crew, defenses or upgrades. That sum is not an arrival schedule.

**Adapt when:** a weapon stops firing after damage or crowding, the enemy closes
past the weapon's useful targeting conditions, or allies enter the hazard area.
Check [repair versus crew replacement](repair-crew-and-reload.md) before spending
on the wrong service. Avoid stacking another nearby M3 without checking spacing.

**Failure cases:** intermittent rocket scans can miss a proximity opportunity;
scattered cannon effects do not guarantee a hit at the enemy center; armor and
crew use different recipients. None of these prices establishes kills per TP.

Jacob's post-2.3 Tank Fortress account motivates thinking about a transition:
infantry/Engineer defenses precede expensive tanks, then medical, repair and
radio dependencies grow. Its reported run beyond wave 200 and Victoria Cross
claim are community anecdotes with no exact tested binary, starting profile or
failed-run series here. Its own explosion/close-enemy warnings matter. They do
not establish this plan's success. [Historical cases and boundaries](strategy-and-versions.md).

## Plan a career unlock and one compatible medal goal

**Goal:** make the next career entry and award legible before choosing purchases.
For a Default start, a useful example is planning toward **First Lieutenant's
rank-gated Airborne entry**, while separately tracking a wave milestone. This is
a planning route, not a promise to earn either in one run.

1. Record current **career rank, career points and relevant award flags** before
   deployment. First Lieutenant's selected point test is **strictly greater than
   3,000** with its preceding-rank/ordered-check conditions. Exactly 3,000 is not
   enough. Soldier XP is not this counter. [Career ranks](career-medals-and-ranks.md#career-rank-reads-points-not-a-soldiers-xp).
2. Choose the particular milestone. **Bronze Star** uses local wave **at least
   40**, not its description's 50. **Survival Ribbon** additionally needs zero
   casualties and mission-total wave **greater than 100**. Local wave and
   mission-total wave are different; choose which you are monitoring.
   [Wave and medal tests](career-medals-and-ranks.md#mission-and-wave-milestones).
3. Keep purchases compatible with the chosen goal. If you instead target the
   **Guerilla warfare Award**, its selected building-plus-vehicle counter sum
   must be exactly zero and mission-total wave greater than 100. The infantry
   wall plan above is incompatible once its construction-counter addition is
   reached; demolition does not reset a historical event counter. Do not merge
   every study into one build order. [Counter-specific exceptions](support-and-economy-awards.md#guerilla-uses-specific-construction-and-vehicle-counters).
4. At mission end, distinguish score, awarded career points and leftover TP.
   Before choosing Airborne later, check the **career-rank-2 named button** and
   its separate attempt-allowance entry. The Airborne badge is another condition,
   not the same thing as an attempt or the rank unlock.
   [Results accounting](score-and-career-points.md), [Airborne entry](deployments.md#an-airborne-attempt-is-not-the-same-state-as-the-badge).

**Adapt when:** a casualty makes a no-casualty goal fail, a purchase changes a
counter-sensitive plan, or a rank/award does not appear at the number expected.
Recheck eligibility, ordered award processing, the actual benefit and saving as
four separate stages. Do not spend more to chase an already-incompatible goal.
[Award troubleshooting](career-medals-and-ranks.md#why-has-a-milestone-not-produced-the-expected-reward).

**Failure cases:** a display is not proof of durable saving; several eligible
awards can wait behind earlier entries; a rank promotion need not jump every
eligible rung. This guide does not qualify a saved profile after reload or an
efficient career-points-per-minute route.
