# Tactical points: different sources have different limits

**Tactical points (TP) pay for recruits, equipment, construction and support.**
If you need both a new recruit and its later job, budget for both using the
[purchase examples](purchase-budgets.md). Soldier XP and career points follow
[separate progression rules](progression.md); do not read one as your TP balance.

Historical scope: selected **2.3.1g** source, not a current modern economy promise.

Find your question: [possible income](#where-can-more-tp-come-from),
[a changed balance or failed order](#why-did-my-balance-change-or-an-order-fail),
[cap behavior](#a-pre-award-check-is-not-an-immediate-post-award-clamp), or
[payment exceptions and refunds](free-orders-and-refunds.md).

There is **no single universal cap established by one 20-TP rule**. Source paths
use different admission thresholds, later clamps and mode conditions. Payment,
income and a cap can also happen in different callbacks.
[TP source evidence](../player-campaign-evidence.md#deployments-and-tp)

[Named award effects](support-and-economy-awards.md) identifies Survival,
Veterancy and Guerilla warfare fields in selected TP expressions, alongside
separate counter-artillery and HQ award consumers.

## Plan spending from the balance you have

**Do not assume a pending action has reserved every later charge or will finish.**
Some offers pay earlier; others check or charge at a later step. Keep that call
or purchase's own payment stages in mind. For example, from a
supplied balance of 8 TP, normal paid Engineer 4 + wall 1 leaves **3 TP** only
if both purchases complete with no other balance changes. Selecting a further
support offer does not reserve that remaining balance against later spending.
This is arithmetic, not a newly observed transaction.
[Recruit-plus-job budgets](purchase-budgets.md), [radio payment stages](support.md).

If TP disappears or an order fails, first identify the last event: a purchase,
placement, accepted radio call, income event or HQ state change. Compare that
specific rule with the actual balance and outcome before trying the purchase
again. A failed delivery is not a general refund, and expected future income is
not money you can spend now. [Payment exceptions](free-orders-and-refunds.md).

For a bigger commitment, compare the [armor-response plan](strategy-studies.md#answer-armor-before-committing-to-a-tank-transition)
with the cost of retaining the infantry position while you save. No income path
below supplies a guaranteed time at which the plan becomes affordable.

## Where can more TP come from?

These are selected historical **2.3.1g** income paths, not a guaranteed income
schedule or a ranking of ways to earn points:

- **Waves and Taunt:** resource additions have their own balance, mode and caller
  checks. Taunt can also request more enemies; a cooldown reset does not prove a
  payment or counted wave. [Wave and Taunt comparison](#manual-taunts-and-automatic-waves-do-not-share-every-rule).
- **Crates:** the selected contents determine the reward. A visible crate is not
  yet collected, and not every outcome is a TP award. [Crate rewards](crates.md).
- **Situation report:** a request can cost TP without awarding TP. Its possible
  reward and cap do not guarantee a net gain. [Report accounting](situation-report.md).
- **Field HQ:** conditional bonus income has a separate recorded-bonus repayment
  risk. More HQs do not automatically multiply that producer's award.
  [HQ conditions](field-hq.md).
- **Downed pilot:** a qualifying friendly pickup can grant TP; an enemy pickup
  instead subtracts TP. A close approach alone is not a completed collection.
  [Pickup eligibility and rewards](pilot-recovery.md).

These paths are useful things to check when your balance changes. The linked
pages retain their exact source conditions and remaining limits. Spending while
a request is pending can still affect its later affordability; expected income
has not already paid for a queued order. [Support accounting](support.md),
[fee waivers and refunds](free-orders-and-refunds.md).

## Why did my balance change, or an order fail?

For **selected historical 2.3.1g**, identify the source of the change before
applying a price, income rule or cap:

- **Buying equipment or placing a structure.** Menu selection and later receipt/completion can be separate. Check the recipient or builder and the later affordability test. [Equipment](training-and-equipment.md), [construction](construction.md)
- **A radio call was pending while I spent TP.** The initial offer need not reserve its payment. Several calls check the balance later; Smoke has two guarded charges and Naval uses the balance at acceptance. [Support accounting](support.md)
- **A wave, crate or HQ changed my balance.** Admission thresholds and later caps differ. A pre-addition balance test is not always an immediate clamp after the award. See the examples below. [Exact producer comparison](../tactical-point-limits.md)
- **I lost TP when the HQ changed state.** Its selected repayment path subtracts a shared recorded bonus; this differs from an ordinary purchase. [Field HQ](field-hq.md)
- **I want to carry TP into Proper Planning.** The selected results action upper-clamps stored leftover to 20; that field is still separate from the next deployment's final balance. [Carryover](score-and-career-points.md#proper-planning-carryover-is-not-every-tp-you-ended-with)

There is no qualified universal cancellation/refund rule or guaranteed income
rate. Budget using the particular transaction rather than assuming all future
TP has already been reserved.

## A pre-award check is not an immediate post-award clamp

Some wave/cadence writes add points only when the **old balance** is below a
threshold. A later helper can then inspect the new balance. For example, with
no cap bonuses and other early caller conditions satisfied, old 18 plus 2 becomes 20;
the following HQ addition cannot pass its strict below 20 gate. Old 17 plus 2 becomes 19,
which can pass that separate HQ gate if its other tests succeed.
[Ordered wave/HQ producers](../tactical-point-limits.md)

A separate crate addition can take 19 to 22 before a later eligible cap write sets
it to 20. That cap has its own source conditions and is not proof that every
point change in every mode is immediately clamped. [Crate source](../tactical-point-limits.md)

## Manual taunts and automatic waves do not share every rule

The selected Taunt button can add 2 below local wave 5 or 1 otherwise, with its own
cooldown, enemy-allocation, mode and old-balance conditions. Its wave helper can
have further resource effects. A menu click, resource addition and displayed
wave increment are not the same event. [Wave callers](waves.md), [Taunt](../taunt.md)

The late-wave income threshold omits one award-dependent term used by several
other producers. Replacing all source thresholds with a nicer uniform formula
would erase a real distinction. [Exact producer table](../tactical-point-limits.md)

## Radio and HQ have separate accounting risks

A rewarded Situation report pays its fee before the reward and applies its own
conditional cap. Smoke has two guarded debit sites. Naval acceptance can consume
the current balance. Field HQ's selected state can repay a shared accumulated
bonus. These are separate paths with specific prerequisites and lifecycles.
[Situation report](situation-report.md), [Smoke](smoke.md),
[Naval support](naval-and-counter-artillery.md), [Field HQ](field-hq.md)

A building's Boolean presence query does not grant one extra income award per
building, and a pending order does not necessarily reserve TP against later
spending. No universal safe-cancellation/refund rule or earned-income rate is
claimed by this overview.

Sources: [Source identities](../player-notes/tactical-points.md#citation-1). Selected 2.3.1g; [exact evidence](../player-campaign-evidence.md).

## Manoeuvre uses one exact counter value

Its selected TP producer increments timer2, then needs the existing Ribbon
flag 1, zero casualties and new timer2 exactly 600 before adding 1. The award's
at-least 600 condition is a different reader; earning it later does not turn
601 into 600. At this addition there is no current-TP threshold or later reset
inside the block, so it cannot define a universal cap or recurring five-minute
income. Counter units and actual callback order remain unqualified.
[Declared 599/600 visits and exact producer](../player-career-award-evidence.md#manoeuvre-eligibility-and-its-tp-reward-have-different-timer-tests).
Additional source: [Source identities](../player-notes/tactical-points.md#citation-2)

A [reached Situation-report comparison](situation-report.md#the-advertised-reward-is-not-expected-tp-income)
also separates a possible jackpot from its fee/reward average. Its XP/career
writes and full radio timing remain other parts of the decision.
