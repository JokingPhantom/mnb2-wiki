# Trench

A **2-TP engineer construction** whose selected template contains an ordinary
cover shape. Against the inspected cover-using enemy firearm paths, a soldier
whose point passes that query gets one **50% save against an otherwise accurate
shot**. Placement must actually satisfy the shape test.
[Protection and combinations](defenses.md)

## Building and positioning

Choose the trench construction order, then complete the placement through the
engineer/build interface. The menu sets a pending cue; it is not a finished
trench. The completion path duplicates the trench at the requested mouse position
and gives it a random rotation from **−10 through +9 degrees**. That rotation is
one reason the visible footprint should not be treated as an exact, fixed cover
stencil. [Construction and shape evidence](../player-defense-evidence.md#structure-bindings)

The trench and sandbag templates both name the same underlying cover-shape
character, transformed differently. Their drawings and price are different, but
putting both around a soldier does not cause the cover helper to return “two.”
A qualifying bunker can add the separate bunker save in the applicable shooter
paths. [Sandbags](sandbags.md), [defense matrix](defenses.md)

## Explosions are a separate question

The community calls trenches permanent bullet protection and warns about
explosions inside. Keep that historical advice distinct from the initialized
flammability flag and from each explosion caller's actual recipient tests.
A successful ordinary-cover query does not automatically activate bunker-only
fragmentation protection. [Trench research](../trenches-and-sandbags.md),
[fragmentation](../fragmentation-blast.md)
