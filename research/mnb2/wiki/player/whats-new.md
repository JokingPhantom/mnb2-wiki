# What's new and corrected

Use this page to find changes that affect a decision while playing. Each entry
names its edition; a modern correction does not rewrite historical rules or an
archived experiment.

## Historical selected 2.3.1g

- **Learning the controls:** the [starting checklist](starting-checklist.md)
  now pairs actions with visible results and recovery advice. The
  [practice purchase](beginner-opening.md) budgets a Garand separately from a
  new recruit. Its November 11 original-game run records a paid purchase and deliberate mission end, with failed attempts and results limitations stated.
- **Troubleshooting movement:** a destination marker records an order, not
  arrival. [Start with the movement checks](controls.md#why-is-a-selected-soldier-not-moving)
  before spending TP on a problem caused by orders or state.
- **Planning the whole job:** [purchase budgets](purchase-budgets.md) combine
  recruits with their construction, weapon or radio costs. Equal totals do not
  mean equal combat value.

## Modern simulation and archived measurements

- **November 7, 2026 combat comparisons:** [combat results](combat-results.md)
  describe stationary, unopposed trials of the named builds and loadouts.
  Later fixes do not retroactively update those timings.
- **Corrections after those studies:** [combat findings](combat-issues.md)
  separates archived failures, later corrected branches and unresolved limits.
  Check that page before carrying an old result into a current loadout.

## How this page stays useful

When a checked article change affects a purchase, control, warning or progression
decision, replace or add a short entry here with its edition and a link to the
answer. Keep the homepage's featured mechanic, tactical case and progression
goal pointed at current useful pages. Use the hosted article's revision history
for exact edits; this page is a small reading guide, not a second acceptance log.
